Install

  1. Switch the box on (power button latched in) and plug its USB-C into this computer with a data cable.
  2. Press the Guru or Furu button and pick the device. It shows as USB JTAG/serial debug unit or cu.usbmodem….
  3. Choose Install and accept the erase prompt. Writing 8 MB takes two or three minutes.
  4. Not found, or it will not connect: hold the Feather's BOOT button, tap RESET, let go of BOOT, then try again.

Read the log

  1. Press either button again and connect. On a box that is already installed, choose Logs & console.
  2. Press Reset device to see it start from the top. Every press, hold and setup change is printed.

What to check

Switched onRing turns blue: setup is open for three minutes. Log says box: GURU … sounds ok.
Slap the domeSound plays, the dome lights while it plays (at least 1.5 s), and the ring cycles colours. Log says press: playing /cheer-1.wav.
Setup closesRing turns green (Guru) or red (Furu). Log says portal: closed, radio off.
Hold the dome 5 sSetup reopens for ten minutes and the ring goes blue.
Hold the dome 15 sAlso forgets the saved Wi-Fi.
Dome blinks three times, over and overThe sounds are missing. Install again from this page.

Pick a sound, set the volume, or join Wi-Fi

While setup is open (ring blue; hold the dome 5 s to reopen it), join the hotspot guru-box or furu-box from a phone and open http://192.168.4.1/box in the phone's real browser. Eleven sounds are built in (three cheers, three boos, a buzzer, a ding, a sad trombone, an air horn and a rimshot), each with a ▶ to hear it first, or upload your own. Wi-Fi is optional: the box makes its sound without it. Stay near the box while a sound uploads; the page shows its progress and says plainly if the connection drops.

Updating a box that is already set up

No cable needed. On the box's setup page, Check for an update fetches the latest over your Wi-Fi (join the box to it first with Configure WiFi), or Install from a file takes the app on its own: guru-app.bin · furu-app.bin. An update keeps the box's sound, volume and Wi-Fi. Boxes installed before 17 October 2026 need one full install from this page first: that build had no room for updates.
