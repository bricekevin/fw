Dodge Ridge Sign

Flash the Dodge Ridge LED trail-map sign in about a minute. No toolchain, no source clone. Lift status comes straight from the resort's own feed; the sign needs only Wi-Fi and power.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Installing needs a secure connection. Open this page over https://.

What happens

  1. Plug the board into a USB port on this computer. Use a data cable — a charge-only cable powers the board but carries no data.
  2. Press Connect & install and pick the board from the list. It usually appears as cu.usbserial-… or CH340.
  3. Choose Install Dodge Ridge Sign, confirm the erase prompt, and wait about a minute.
  4. When the board reboots, join the open Wi-Fi network SETUP DODGE RIDGE from your phone and enter your home Wi-Fi. That's it — the lifts light up within a minute.

What the colours mean

  • green — open
  • blue — on hold or weather hold
  • red — closed
  • white — no data from the feed

The button on the back

A quick tap steps through brightness. Press and hold and the strip fills up as a warning — keep holding and the sign forgets its Wi-Fi and reopens SETUP DODGE RIDGE for setup.

After the first install

It updates itself over Wi-Fi. Every six hours it checks for a newer build and installs it, rolling back on its own if one fails to start. You only need this page for a brand-new board or a recovery flash.
