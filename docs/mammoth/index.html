Install Mammoth Sign

Flash the Mammoth Mountain LED trail-map sign in about a minute — no toolchain, no source clone. All 25 lifts light straight from Mammoth's own public feed; the sign needs nothing but Wi-Fi and power.

  1. Plug the sign's board into a USB port on this computer (data cable, not charge-only).
  2. Click Connect below and pick the device from the list (its name looks like cu.usbserial-… or CH340).
  3. Click Install Mammoth Sign on the panel that appears, confirm the erase prompt, and wait (~1 min).
  4. When the board reboots, join the open Wi-Fi network SETUP MAMMOTH SIGN from your phone and enter your home Wi-Fi. That's it — the lifts light up within a minute.
What the colours mean: green — open blue — on hold or weather hold red — closed white — no data from the feed
The switch on the back picks the season feed: up (or not fitted) is the full winter mountain, down is the short summer list. Flip it any time — the sign picks it up on its next check, no power cycle.
Browser: works in Chrome or Edge on desktop (they have WebSerial). Safari and Firefox don't yet — open this page in Chrome.
After the first install the sign updates itself over Wi-Fi — every six hours it checks for a newer release and rolls it in automatically. You only need this page for a brand-new board or a recovery flash.
